My dear Miss Penny,

We returned safely to Great Malvern yesterday evening after our delightful visit with you all
at the Rectory in Wolverhampton. Edward joins with me in sending our warmest thanks to your
father and yourself for your kind hospitality and the pleasant hours we spent together.
The weather was most agreeable, and Edward has already returned to his study to work on his
musical sketches. We hope you will not forget your promise to come and visit us here in Malvern
when the summer draws to a close.

With our united kindest regards and affectionate remembrances to your father,

Believe me,
Yours very sincerely,
Caroline Alice Elgar
